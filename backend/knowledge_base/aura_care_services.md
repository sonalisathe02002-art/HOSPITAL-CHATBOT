# AuraCare Services and Clinical Support

## Specialist Departments
The hospital offers specialist care in cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, pediatrics, and general medicine. Each department works with trained nurses and diagnostic support staff to coordinate treatment plans and follow-up appointments.

## Healthcare Support Services
- High-resolution MRI and CT imaging
- Ultrasound and pathology labs
- In-house pharmacy with 24/7 access
- Robotic surgery support and perioperative care
- Telehealth follow-up for selected non-emergency cases

## Patient Experience
Patients are encouraged to check appointment reminders, insurance eligibility, and specialist instructions before arrival. The hospital prioritizes patient privacy, respectful communication, and coordinated care across departments.

## Emergency Service Guidelines
For chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden weakness, severe bleeding, or other life-threatening symptoms, emergency rooms provide immediate care and triage. Patients should not delay emergency treatment while waiting for a scheduled appointment.

## Common Support Questions
- Visiting hours and emergency access are listed in the patient guide.
- Parking and valet information is posted at the main entrance and digital patient portal.
- Appointment changes can be handled through the hospital portal or reception desk.
- Insurance coverage details are confirmed during registration and billing.
