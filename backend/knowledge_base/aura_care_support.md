# AuraCare Hospital Support Knowledge Base

## Hospital Overview
AuraCare Medical & Surgical Center is a private multi-specialty hospital located at 100 Medical Center Blvd, Metro Health District. The hospital includes inpatient wards, ICU, diagnostic imaging, surgery suites, pharmacy, and a 24/7 emergency department.

## Visiting Hours
- General Inpatient Wards: 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM, daily.
- Intensive Care Unit (ICU): 4:30 PM to 5:30 PM daily, restricted to immediate family members.
- Outpatient Clinics: Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- Emergency Department: Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Parking and Campus Access
- Multi-level patient parking is available in P1, P2, and P3.
- Parking is free for the first two hours for patients and visitors.
- Complimentary valet parking is available at the main entrance.
- The main reception desk is on the ground floor, and the emergency department is on the ground floor in Wing A.

## Appointment Booking
Patients can book appointments online using the patient portal. The process includes selecting a department, choosing a specialist doctor, selecting a date, choosing an available time slot, and confirming the request. Same-day urgent consults may be offered depending on specialty availability.

## Departments and Services
- Cardiology
- Neurology
- Orthopedics
- Pediatrics
- General Medicine
- Diagnostic Imaging
- Pharmacy
- Emergency and Trauma Care

## Insurance and Billing
AuraCare accepts Blue Cross Blue Shield, Medicare, Medicaid, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, and Kaiser Permanente. Standard specialist consultation fees vary by doctor and specialty. Payment options include major credit cards, debit cards, HSA/FSA cards, and flexible payment plans.

## Patient Support and Safety
- Patients should bring a government-issued ID and insurance card if available.
- Please arrive 15 minutes early for appointments and check in at the front desk.
- Family visitors may be limited in the ICU to one person at a time.
- For urgent medical emergencies, call 911 or visit the emergency department immediately.
